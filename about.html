Corporate Profile

Who we are

Established in 2015, Genesis Trading Co. has become Bangladesh's leading healthcare technology supplier, delivering diagnostic systems, life support, and dialysis solutions.

Corporate History

A newer approach to health technology

Genesis Trading Co. represents the next generation of clinical infrastructure support. We operate as a high-end medical equipment importer, installation contractor, and logistics provider, bridging international manufacturers and domestic clinics.

Over the past decade, we have established distribution channels representing 90+ renowned brands and executing contracts across hundreds of public and private medical colleges and district hospitals.

Quality Procurement: Verified CE, FDA, and ISO standards compliant systems.
Lifecycle Support: Direct supply chain of reagents and parts.

Distributor credentials

Global Brand Coverage

Regulatory Alignment
